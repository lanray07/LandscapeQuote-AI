# Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 15, 2026

LandscapeQuote AI is an iOS app for creating landscaping estimates.

## Photos

Site photos selected from the camera or photo library are stored locally on your device. In this version, photos are not uploaded to a developer server and are not sent to an external AI service.

## Client Details

Client names, contact details, site addresses, project notes, line items, pricing, and quote status are stored locally on your device using Apple's on-device persistence.

## Quote Data

Saved estimates, PDF exports, contractor notes, and quote totals remain under your control on your device. If future versions add cloud sync or AI API processing, the app should explain what is transferred and ask for any required permission.

## Payments

Subscriptions are handled through Apple's StoreKit in-app purchase flow. The iOS app does not show external payment links.

## Data Collection

This version does not collect analytics, advertising identifiers, tracking data, or developer-hosted account data.
